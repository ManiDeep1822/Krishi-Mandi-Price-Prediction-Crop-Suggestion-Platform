Executive Summary & Abstract

Core Engineering Innovation: Integrating live Agmarknet wholesale data feeds with hybrid AI decision logic (Google Gemini LLM + ICAR agronomic calibrations), real-time logistics profitability algorithms, and GIGW 3.0 government-grade accessibility in a single cloud-native Next.js platform.

Agricultural markets across India are governed by Agricultural Produce Market Committees (APMCs). While wholesale prices fluctuate dynamically based on regional arrivals, seasonal demand, and weather volatility, Indian smallholder farmers frequently suffer from severe market opacity and asymmetric information. Farmers typically harvest simultaneously and sell in the nearest local yard without awareness of modal price trends, inter-mandi price arbitrage, or holding economics, often resulting in distressed sales below Minimum Support Prices (MSP).

The Krishi Mandi Price Prediction & Crop Suggestion Platform addresses this critical socio-economic challenge through an enterprise-grade, farmer-centric web portal. Built in strict compliance with GIGW 3.0 (Guidelines for Indian Government Websites) and WCAG 2.1 AA standards, the system accepts a farmer's location (State, District), primary crop, soil type, agricultural season, and landholding size. It dynamically evaluates:

  • Current Mandi Rate Intelligence: Real-time wholesale arrivals, min/modal/max prices, and intra-day price spreads aggregated from official Government of India Open Government Data (OGD) Agmarknet endpoints.
  • Predictive Price Analytics: Recharts-powered 4-week forward price corridor envelopes, seasonal arrival trendlines, and official MSP floor comparisons.
  • Nearby Markets & Net Realized Profit Matrix: Haulage logistics calculator comparing 30+ regional APMCs, computing road distance, vehicle fuel consumption across 3 fleet categories (Tractor Trolley, Pickup, Commercial Truck), and net realized profits after freight deductions.
  • Hybrid AI Agronomic Advisory: Dual-tier decision engine combining Google Gemini generative AI with localized ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research) models to deliver actionable harvest/selling windows (Strong Hold vs. Staggered Sale vs. Immediate Liquidation), storage cost amortizations, and soil-tailored crop diversification tables.
  • Statutory APMC Gate Entry Pass: A single-page A4 printable pass slip incorporating an offline-scannable 2D QR Code matrix and a concentric administrative APMC rubber ink seal.

1. Introduction & Problem Statement

1.1 Background & Agricultural Context

Agriculture provides livelihood for over 58% of India's population. In the regulated agricultural supply chain, produce is traded across more than 2,400 APMC principal markets and 4,300 sub-market yards across the country. Prices at each APMC yard are determined through daily open auctions based on physical arrivals, buyer demand, commodity variety, moisture levels, and grade.

1.2 Problem Statement & Existing Deficiencies

Despite government digitization initiatives (such as Agmarknet and e-NAM), Indian farmers consistently face critical obstacles:

  • Information Asymmetry & Local Monopolies: Middlemen and commission agents frequently exploit farmers' lack of real-time market knowledge, quoting depressed prices during peak morning harvest arrivals.
  • Absence of Predictive Price Forecasting: Existing portals display only retrospective historical prices, giving farmers zero visibility into whether holding a commodity for 2 to 4 weeks would yield a higher net margin after accounting for warehouse costs.
  • Hidden Logistics Friction: A neighboring APMC yard located 45 km away may offer ₹300 more per quintal, but without calculated vehicle haulage costs (tractor vs. pickup vs. commercial truck), farmers cannot determine whether traveling further is profitable.
  • Unusable interfaces & Language Barriers: Traditional government websites are cluttered, desktop-bound, not optimized for rural mobile viewports, and lack bilingual accessibility.

2. Literature Review & Comparative Analysis

2.1 Limitations of Existing Public & Commercial Systems

A comprehensive review of existing agricultural platforms in India reveals distinct functional bottlenecks:

  • Agmarknet (NIC / DMI Portal): Contains authoritative daily wholesale price ledgers but lacks predictive modeling, responsive mobile layouts, and agronomic crop diversification recommendations.
  • e-NAM (National Agriculture Market): Facilitates electronic trading across connected mandis, yet requires complex KYC registration and does not offer automated freight net-realized profit optimization for individual farmers.
  • Kisan Suvidha App: Provides weather and dealer directories, but lacks integrated holding-cost analysis and official printable gate pass slips.

4.2 Data Flow Diagram (DFD Level 0 & Level 1)

Level 0 Context Diagram: The farmer inputs agricultural parameters into the system. The platform communicates with Agmarknet OGD and Google Gemini APIs, returning validated price ledgers, predictive forecasts, and verified entry slips to the user.

Level 1 Decomposition:

  1. Process 1.0 (Profile Validation): Validates State, District, Crop, Soil, Season, and Acreage against `CROPS_CATALOG` and `SOIL_TYPES`.
  2. Process 2.0 (Price Aggregation): Dispatches sanitized request to `/api/mandi`, parses XML/JSON records from Data.gov.in, maps Min/Modal/Max values, and computes MSP spread.
  3. Process 3.0 (Logistics Optimization): Traverses 30+ mandis in `DISTRICT_MAP`, calculates Euclidean road distance, multiplies by vehicle freight multiplier, and derives net profit.
  4. Process 4.0 (AI Advisory Generation): Constructs structured system prompt with farm metrics, queries Gemini API, validates JSON keys, or activates the ICAR agronomic ruleset on timeout.

5. Detailed Engineering Implementation

5.1 Farm Profile & Land Parameter Engine (`components/FarmProfileForm.jsx`)

Provides a streamlined government registry form (`NIC-AGRI-01`). When the user selects a State (e.g., Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan), the district selector immediately updates with regional markets. Selected crop commodities automatically bind to their respective Government Minimum Support Price (MSP), ensuring seamless baseline calculation.

5.2 Real-Time Mandi Aggregator (`components/LiveMandiWatch.jsx` & `/api/mandi`)

Aggregates live wholesale price arrivals from the Data.gov.in OGD portal. Features include:

  • Intra-day price spread calculation (`Max Price - Min Price`).
  • Percentage deviation against MSP (`Modal vs. MSP`).
  • Client-side search and filtering across market yards, grades, and varieties.
  • One-click CSV Export (`exportToCSV`) and official print bulletin trigger.

5.3 4-Week Predictive Price Corridor (`components/PriceTrendsAnalytics.jsx`)

Visualizes 30-day historical wholesale price movements and daily arrival volumes (in quintals) using Recharts. A secondary projection pipeline evaluates arrival trends to establish a **4-week forward price corridor** (e.g., +1W, +2W, +3W, +4W) bounded by a 95% historical variance envelope.

5.4 Geospatial Freight & Realized Net Profit Matrix (`components/NearbyMandiComparison.jsx`)

Empowers farmers to evaluate price arbitrage across 30+ regional markets. The user toggles between 3 realistic haulage options:

Vehicle Category Payload Capacity Fuel & Haulage Rate Target Farmers
Tractor Trolley 40 Quintals (~4 MT) ₹12 / km Marginal & Smallholder Farmers
Light Commercial Pickup 20 Quintals (~2 MT) ₹8 / km High-speed perishable transport
Commercial Heavy Truck 100 Quintals (~10 MT) ₹22 / km FPOs & Large Farm Cooperatives

5.5 Hybrid AI Decision Support System (`components/AIAdvisoryPanel.jsx` & `/api/advisory`)

The platform adopts an enterprise hybrid AI paradigm. If an active Gemini API key is configured, a senior agronomic prompt is evaluated by Google's generative models (`gemini-3.8-flash`, `gemini-3.7-flash`, `gemini-flash-latest`), returning a validated JSON response. If the network is restricted, the platform immediately invokes its calibrated ICAR agronomic ruleset:

5.6 Statutory APMC Gate Entry Pass with Dual Seal (`components/GatePassModal.jsx`)

To eliminate paperwork delays at crowded mandi checkpoints, the platform generates a formal APMC Mandi Yard Gate Pass featuring:

  • 100% Real Scannable 2D QR Code: Encoded with comprehensive plain-text vehicle, farmer, commodity, and security data that scans instantly with standard smartphone cameras or optical barcode readers at the weighbridge.
  • Authentic Concentric Rubber Stamp: Rendered in classic administrative violet/purple (`#4338ca`) indelible ink with a subtle -3° physical tilt, double concentric rings, star delimiters, and Hindi/English arc typography.
  • Single-Page A4 Print Isolation: Strict CSS `@page` and `@media print` rules completely suppress the background dashboard, guaranteeing a clean 1-page slip.

6. Security, GIGW 3.0 & Governance

6.1 Security Architecture & Hardening

Threat Vector Vulnerability Risk Mitigation Mechanism
DoS / Scraping Abuse Resource exhaustion on serverless routes Sliding-window IP rate limiting in `lib/rateLimit.js` enforcing 60 req/min for mandi and 10 req/min for AI.
Prompt Injection Adversarial input breaking out of LLM JSON schema Defensive sanitization stripping control tokens, newlines, and brackets before feeding prompts to Gemini.
Unauthorized Settings Mod Tampering with server-wide API keys Mandatory `ADMIN_SECRET_KEY` authentication header verification on `/api/config`.
Secret Leakage API credentials committed to GitHub `.gitignore` strictly isolates `.env*.local`. Zero keys committed to version control.

6.2 GIGW 3.0 Government Web Compliance

  • Three-State Font Scaler: Implements accessible font sizing (`A-`, `A`, `A+`) dynamically modifying the root `html[data-font-size]` property.
  • Skip to Main Content: Accessible keyboard anchor (`.skip-link`) allowing screen readers to bypass navigation bars.
  • Institutional Color Palette: Deep Royal Navy (`#162f6a`), Saffron Gold (`#ff9933`), and Agriculture Emerald Green (`#138808`), reflecting official GovTech portal standards.

9. Conclusion & Future Scope

9.1 Conclusion

The Krishi Mandi Price Prediction & Crop Suggestion Platform successfully bridge the digital divide for Indian agriculture. By combining official Agmarknet wholesale data with predictive price corridors, logistics arbitrage calculations, and ICAR-backed AI advisories, the platform empowers farmers to transition from distress selling to strategic, data-driven crop commercialization. The system adheres to strict software engineering standards, GIGW 3.0 accessibility rules, and security guidelines, establishing an industry-ready digital public good.

9.2 Future Enhancements Roadmap

  • Multilingual Voice Assistant: Integration of automated voice input in regional dialects (Marathi, Telugu, Punjabi, Hindi) for low-literacy farmers.
  • Satellite Soil Moisture Telemetry: Ingestion of ISRO Bhuvan or Sentinel-2 satellite imagery for automated soil health estimation.
  • Electronic Negotiable Warehouse Receipts (e-NWR) Integration: Direct online pledging of stored produce with nationalized banks under 7% interest subvention schemes.
  • WhatsApp & SMS Push Alerts: Micro-updates informing farmers when their target commodity reaches a 30-day peak at nearby mandis.
